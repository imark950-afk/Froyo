Privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

In short: we only collect what we need to run your booking and your stamp card. We don’t sell your information, we don’t show ads, and the app has no tracking or analytics. You can delete your account from the app at any time.

Who we are

This app is run by Froyo on the go, a mobile frozen yogurt business with an indoor events cart and a mobile trailer. We are responsible for the personal information collected through the app at app.froyoonthego.com and in our iPhone and Android apps.

Questions or requests about your information: email froyoontap@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

Your accountYour email address and the name you give us, so you can sign in with a one-time code and we know who you are. We don’t use passwords. While you’re signed in, the sign-in service also records your device’s IP address and browser type, to keep your account secure.
BookingsYour name, email, phone number, venue, postcode, event date and time, guest numbers, package, extras and any notes you add. We use these to plan and run your event, contact you about it, and keep records for our accounts.
Stamp cardYour member number, stamps, free cups and a history of when and where stamps were added or redeemed. If you choose to add your birthday (day and month), we use it for your birthday treat.
Staff accountsFor our team only: their role, and when they choose to share where the trailer is serving, that location. This location is the trailer’s, shown publicly on Find us; we don’t collect customers’ locations.

Our legal reasons for using this information under UK data protection law are: to provide the service you ask for (your account, bookings and stamp card), our legitimate interest in running the business and preventing misuse, and legal obligations such as keeping financial records.

Cookies and storage

We set one cookie, on auth.froyoonthego.com, which keeps you signed in. It is secure, can’t be read by other websites, and is removed when you sign out. We don’t use advertising or analytics cookies, and the app doesn’t store your information on your device beyond that.

Who else handles your information

We use a small number of service providers to run the app. They only process information on our instructions:

  • Neon (Neon, Inc.): our database and sign-in service. Your account, bookings and stamp card are stored in its London data centre (run on Amazon Web Services). Neon also sends your sign-in code emails. Sign-in requests pass through a small relay we run on Neon in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): hosts the app’s web pages. Like any website host, it receives your device’s IP address when the app loads.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC): supplies the app’s fonts, so Google receives your device’s IP address when the app loads.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Where information leaves the UK, it is protected by the providers’ data transfer safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US data bridge.

When you tap Directions or Map, your maps app opens; what it collects is covered by its own privacy policy. We don’t take card payments in the app, so we never see your card details.

We don’t sell or rent your information and don’t share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and stamp card: until you delete your account.
  • Bookings: as long as needed to run the event, then for as long as the law requires us to keep business records (usually six years). When you delete your account, bookings that have already happened keep only the reference, date and amounts, with your name and contact details removed. Bookings that hadn’t happened yet keep their contact details; email us to have them removed after the event.
  • Sign-in codes: expire after a few minutes.

Deleting your account

Sign in, tap your initial at the top right, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your sign-in, stamp card and stamp history straight away. Upcoming bookings still go ahead; see How long we keep it for what happens to your bookings. You can also ask us to delete your account by email.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive it in a portable format. Email froyoontap@gmail.com and we’ll reply within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Security

Sign-in uses one-time codes instead of passwords. All connections are encrypted, the database only accepts requests through checked actions, and staff only see what their role needs.

Children

The app is for adults booking events and for stamp card members. Under-13s shouldn’t create an account; a parent or guardian can book or collect stamps for them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes, we’ll let you know in the app.
